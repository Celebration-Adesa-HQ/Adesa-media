# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Organizations seeking an integrated marketing, media, public relations, digital, or production partner in Nigeria.
- Marketing and creative professionals interested in future opportunities with Adesa Media.

## Product Purpose

Adesa Media presents its services, work, approach, people, and company information online. The careers experience gives prospective candidates a truthful view of relevant disciplines, publishes verified vacancies when available, and provides a general-application path when no roles are open.

## Positioning

Adesa Media combines strategy, creative execution, media planning and buying, public relations, digital marketing, production, and experiential work in one full-service agency.

## Operating Context

The company operates from Lagos, Nigeria. Prospective candidates browse the public website, review genuine openings, and apply by email with a CV and relevant portfolio information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The website uses Next.js App Router, Tailwind CSS, and the existing shared navigation and footer.
- Careers content is managed locally in the site configuration.
- General applications use an editable prefilled email addressed to `career@adesahq.com`.
- The first careers release has no verified open vacancies and must not fabricate roles.
- JobPosting structured data is emitted only for configured real positions.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Adesa Media name, existing logo assets, navy and orange identity, and direct professional voice.
- Use positive recruiting language without implying that a vacancy exists when none is configured.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing service disciplines, company values, Lagos office details, portfolio work, and contact details in `config/site.jsx`.
- No verified vacancies, employee testimonials, recruiting photography, benefits, salaries, or hiring timelines are currently available.

## Product Principles

- Be specific about the work and honest about hiring status.
- Keep the action clear for both vacancy applicants and general applicants.
- Reuse verified company facts instead of inventing recruitment claims.
- Keep content maintainable through centralized configuration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The careers route must remain responsive, keyboard accessible, readable at high zoom, and compatible with reduced-motion preferences.
